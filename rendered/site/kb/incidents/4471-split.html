The 5 one-day reports are filed under parser by tenant, and counted there too.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so the day and the month change places. This is the fix that #4471 queued.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the date day first. Only booking-v3 tenants, only through the form. The parser fix does not repair it: the hint must change.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, and the weekday changes with it. One meeting shows two different days in Lisbon and in Toronto. A day/month swap never does this. It points to a time-zone or a DST conversion. The desk noted these reports (desk-notes.json), but the kb still filed them under parser by tenant.

How the count works

  • One id is one report. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice: the table counts each id once.
  • The intake tag is not the cause. Intake guesses from the browser locale and never looks up the tenant. The cause comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts).
  • The counts are the desk’s view, as exported: each report counts where the desk filed it. The one-day moves come from the desk’s own reading of the report text (desk-notes.json). They stay filed under parser too, so the parser count includes them.

What to do next

  • Close #4471 as “split”. Track each problem on its own ticket.
  • Merge new date reports by cause, not by subject line. The subject line put three problems in one ticket.
